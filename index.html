Option A

Original layout

The sheet as chosen, nothing added.

The Foundation sits in a full-bleed blue band with its two green dots already filled at press time, so the sheet arrives with the foundation prescribed before the NP writes a word. Green ink appears nowhere else, which means a green dot can only ever mean prescribed.

Pathway rows run at 38pt, the most generous of any version, and each carries a plain-language line the patient can re-read at home.

  • Roomiest pathway rows of the two.
  • No space for anything the printed fields do not cover.
  • Simplest to print and the least to go wrong.

Option B

With notes

Same sheet, plus two ruled lines for the visit.

Identical to Option A except for a ruled Notes block under the last pathway, for anything the visit turned up that no printed field covers.

Concept 01 has almost no spare vertical space, so the Notes block is paid for: rows compress from 38pt to 33pt, with small trims to the foundation band and the sign-off. That is the whole trade, writing room against row breathing room.

  • Two 24pt ruled lines, normal handwriting height.
  • Rows at 33pt rather than 38pt.
  • Closer to how the current pad works, which has an “Other” line.

The Formulary

4 pages · 111 entries

What the pad prescribes from. NP-facing, never a patient handout.

Supplements lead the document, because they are the foundation. All 34 come off the current pad in its own eight categories. Hormones and peptides follow, then infusions and procedures, then memberships and open items.

The IV, peptide, ozone and regenerative lists were pulled from wellinfused.com rather than the handoff, which was incomplete: peptides went from 2 named compounds to 13, and ozone and regenerative gained named protocols they did not have before.

Anything not yet supplied appears as a dashed placeholder row rather than being left out, so gaps read as gaps.

  • Page 1 — The Foundation. 34 supplements in 8 categories.
  • Page 2 — Hormones and peptides, split apart.
  • Page 3 — 10 infusions, 4 lab-driven protocols, injections, ozone, salt room, regenerative.
  • Page 4 — Memberships, packages, next appointments, and 11 open placeholders.

Open before anything prints

Carried forward, plus what the formulary surfaced.

  • Naming collision. “Functional Fusion” is live on wellinfused.com as a specific lab-driven IV protocol, the 3-Series and 6-Series, and is also the name of the whole framework. Both appear in this formulary, on page 3, a few lines apart. One of them needs to change.
  • Pricing on a clinical document. Membership and package pricing is on page 4 because it is on the current pad. Billing period is not stated there: Foundation at $49 against Reserve at $849 cannot be the same interval. Confirm the intervals, and confirm price belongs here at all.
  • Duplicate entries. BPC-157 is a supplement on the current pad (GI Stack, and with TB-500) and a peptide on the website. Confirm which list owns it.
  • Three live services are excluded. Weight Loss & Body Composition, Joint & Muscle Therapy and Breast Thermography are all on the website but were tabled in the handoff. They are listed on page 4 under “Not in this protocol” so the omission is deliberate and visible. Confirm they stay out.
  • Logo files. The wave mark was rebuilt as clean vector from the brand PDF, but the wordmark is set in live Avenir. Request the vector files before a print run.
